# 01 | Introduction: From Raw Sequencing Reads to a Gene Expression Count Matrix

## Overview

Welcome to the hands-on section of the Single-Cell RNA-seq Analysis Workshop!

Single-cell RNA sequencing (scRNA-seq) allows us to study gene expression at the level of individual cells. Unlike bulk RNA-seq, which measures the average gene expression across a population of cells, scRNA-seq enables us to investigate cellular heterogeneity, identify distinct cell populations, and explore cell states and biological processes.

In this tutorial, we will learn how raw sequencing data are processed to generate a gene expression count matrix, which is the starting point for most downstream scRNA-seq analyses.

We will use Cell Ranger, a software package developed by 10x Genomics, to process sequencing data from a publicly available human peripheral blood mononuclear cell (PBMC) dataset.

No prior experience with Linux or command-line tools is required.
